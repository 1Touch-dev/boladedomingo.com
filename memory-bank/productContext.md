# Product context

Bola de Domingo exists so a reader in Brazil can follow the Sunday of football without a federation bulletin and without a club's official voice.

## Problem

Official league channels do not describe the Sunday the desk actually signs: the largo in São Paulo, the campinho in Belo Horizonte, the afternoon circuit, the morning cup that must not leak into the taça. Community groups invent scores the desk cannot sign. The Brasileirão, the Copa do Brasil, and the Libertadores are official competitions; this desk does not reprint them.

## How it should work

The home page carries the desk sections: date line, masthead, score ticker, lead note, latest notes, upcoming fixtures, the desk's own Taça de Domingo table, and a category index. The newsletter sits on an asphalt band above a three-column footer, and again under the home article grid. Both post to the Brazil CMS.

Scores appear only when this desk signed them, between its own praças and tardes. The seleção week is a note, not a result. Rosters never appear.

## Experience

- One language: Portuguese (`pt-BR`).
- Search (Ctrl/Cmd+K) covers praças, tardes, and notes.
- The page is a sports-news front: dark bar, masthead, ticker, lead story, table. It should not read as the Guanabara bay sheet, the Gávea hillside sheet, or a club supporter site.
